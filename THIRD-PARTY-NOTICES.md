ImageMagick is distributed under the ImageMagick license. The bundled executable is obtained
from the official ImageMagick release artifacts:

<https://github.com/ImageMagick/ImageMagick/releases>

<https://imagemagick.org/script/license.php>

## sharp / libvips / libheif

`sharp` distributes prebuilt `libvips` components. Those components may include `libheif`.
Their license texts and notices are included in the installed npm packages under
`node_modules/sharp` and `node_modules/@img/`.

The project does not modify these components. Release bundles must retain the corresponding
package notices alongside this file.
